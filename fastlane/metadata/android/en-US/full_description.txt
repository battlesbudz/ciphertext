<h1>Ciphertext</h1>

<p>Ephemeral 1:1 live-session messenger. No user IDs, no accounts, no history that outlives the conversation.</p>

<p>Ciphertext is a fork of SimpleX Chat, licensed under AGPLv3. It is compatible with the SimpleX network: messages travel over SimpleX relay servers, but Ciphertext adds a strict ephemeral session model on top. When a session ends, for any reason, everything is wiped and both sides start a brand new session. Nothing carries over.</p>

<ul>
<li>1:1 live sessions only. A session exists only while both parties are connected.</li>
<li>Disconnect kills the session. Clean exit, network blip, or heartbeat timeout: the session dies, messages, keys, and relay state are destroyed.</li>
<li>Fresh reconnect. Both clients automatically establish a brand new session with fresh keys. Nothing is preserved.</li>
<li>10 second grace window when you leave the app, for the image picker or a brief app switch. Still gone after 10 seconds and the session is wiped on both sides.</li>
<li>Heartbeats and session invites never appear in the chat.</li>
<li>Optional Tor routing through Orbot, one tap in Settings, Network and servers.</li>
<li>No user IDs of any kind. No phone numbers, no emails, no handles.</li>
<li>Your profile lives on your device and is shared only with your contacts.</li>
</ul>

<p>Install from GitHub Releases. Ciphertext runs no servers. Sessions are ephemeral and wiped on disconnect. See the privacy policy in the repo.</p>
